Find your chair

There’s no wrong door.

A few gentle questions to help you land in the right place. It takes about a minute, nothing is stored, and it isn’t an assessment — just a way of pointing you at the chair that fits. You can stop at any point and simply book.

Question 1 of 4

Who are we keeping a chair for?

Whoever you’re here for, they’re welcome.

Question 2 of 4

What’s bringing you here?

Pick the closest one. These overlap in real life, and they’re never treated separately here — so you don’t have to get this exactly right.

Question 3 of 4

Where would you rather sit?

Telehealth now, and the Nedlands room in Perth. Tell me which suits you.

Question 4 of 4

How would you like to start?

There’s no commitment in any of these. You can change your mind after.

Your starting point

Don’t want to answer questions? That’s completely fine — book a session directly, or just ask us a question.
A note on what this is

These questions aren’t an assessment, a screening tool or a diagnosis — they only help point you to the right page. Nothing you tap is saved or sent to us. If you need urgent support right now, please contact your GP, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or call 000 in an emergency.

Body Belonging Clinic acknowledges Country.

Based in Boorloo on Whadjuk Noongar Country, with telehealth support across Australia. We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters we work on, and pay respect to Elders past and present. We recognise that body, eating, health, identity, culture and belonging are connected, and that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples hold continuing relationships to Country, community, kin and culture.

Sovereignty was never ceded. Always was, always will be Aboriginal land.
