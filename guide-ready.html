A gentle note

This guide is a starting point, not a substitute for support.

It’s general information, not therapy, and not a diagnosis. When you’d like a real conversation, you’re warmly welcome to book a session or email the clinic. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 support: Lifeline 13 11 14, 13YARN 13 92 76, Kids Helpline 1800 55 1800, or the Butterfly Foundation 1800 33 4673.
