Referral pathways

Medicare & EDP

Medicare rebates may be available with a valid GP Mental Health Treatment Plan, psychiatrist or paediatrician referral. As an ANZAED Credentialed Eating Disorder Clinician, Lauren can also work under an eligible Eating Disorder Treatment and Management Plan, giving eligible clients access to up to 40 Medicare-rebated sessions a year, against 10 under a standard plan, where criteria are met. From 1 November 2025, a Mental Health Treatment Plan must be prepared by the patient’s MyMedicare-registered or usual GP practice for rebates to apply. Body Belonging is also a registered DVA provider: eligible veterans and their families can be referred for DVA-funded mental health sessions.

Access

Nedlands + telehealth

Appointments in Nedlands and by telehealth across Australia, including Quick Chair — a 25-minute bulk-billed telehealth session (MBS 91175) for eligible clients with a valid referral.

Our referrer standard

We close the loop on every referral.

Every referral is confirmed within 48 hours, and we contact your patient directly to arrange a first appointment. You receive a written update at the start and close of care — and at plan review points — with direct access to Lauren, not a call centre. And because Body Belonging doesn’t assess ADHD or autism, there’s no diagnostic overlap with your own work.

Why an AMHSW

Government-endorsed credential

Accredited Mental Health Social Workers hold a credential assessed by the AASW through a process endorsed by the Australian Government, and are Medicare-recognised providers of Focussed Psychological Strategies under Better Access and the EDP. Rebates are the same as for registered psychologists and occupational therapists delivering FPS. Body Belonging is also a registered DVA provider, recognised under WorkCover WA, and social work is a funded therapy support under the NDIS.

In step with WA’s direction

Community care, before crisis

The WA Eating Disorders Framework 2025–2030 calls for earlier, community-based eating disorder care and stronger primary-care pathways. Body Belonging adds specialist community capacity on exactly that front — by telehealth across Australia, with in-person sessions in Nedlands from November 2026.

Evidence

The overlap is documented

Peer-reviewed research links ADHD and autism with substantially higher rates of eating disorders and disordered eating, and ARFID co-occurs strongly with both — the overlap this practice was built for.

What to include

What helps in a referral.

When available, please include the client’s preferred name, pronouns, date of birth, contact details, Medicare referral pathway, current supports, relevant medical information, eating or body image concerns, risk concerns, cultural or accessibility considerations, and whether family, kin, carers or other practitioners are involved.

For urgent risk, medical instability or crisis concerns, please use emergency or acute services. Body Belonging Clinic is not an emergency or crisis service.
