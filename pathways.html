Pathways

Rarely just one thing.

ADHD, eating, body image, identity and recovery are tangled together in real life — so they’re never treated separately here. We hold the overlap, and the whole story fits in one room.

Body Belonging Clinic acknowledges Country.

Based in Boorloo on Whadjuk Noongar Country, with telehealth support across Australia. We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters we work on, and pay respect to Elders past and present. We recognise that body, eating, health, identity, culture and belonging are connected, and that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples hold continuing relationships to Country, community, kin and culture.

Sovereignty was never ceded. Always was, always will be Aboriginal land.
