Coming in 2027

You got the diagnosis. Then everyone went home.

After the Diagnosis is an eight-week group for adults with ADHD, in person in Nedlands. It’s the scaffolding that comes next. How to eat, sleep, decide and ask for help in ways that actually fit you.

Nothing is running yet. I’m asking who would come before I build it. Putting your name down costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

What it will look like

Practical. Small. Built for ADHD brains.

The format

Eight weeks, in person

Ninety minutes a week in Nedlands, with six to eight people. Fidgets on the table, permission to stand, and babies welcome.

What we cover

The everyday stuff that falls apart

Eating, sleep, decisions, feelings that arrive turned up loud, big life changes, and asking for help by name. Skills from CBT, DBT and ACT, built around a simple map of your day called Signal & Capacity.

Medication

It works either way

Medication is a decision for you and your prescriber. This group is everything around it, and it never asks anyone to change what they’re doing.

Cost

Medicare-rebated

$70 a session. With a Mental Health Treatment Plan from your GP, Medicare pays $22.70 back, so it’s $47.30 out of pocket. Group sessions are counted separately from your individual sessions. A limited number of concession places are available in every group.

Questions

Good to know.

When does it start?

In 2027. The exact dates depend on how many people put their name down and which times suit them. Everyone on the list hears first.

Do I need a referral to put my name down?

No. When a group is confirmed, you’ll need a Mental Health Treatment Plan from your GP to claim the Medicare rebate. Everyone also has a one-to-one chat with me before the group starts, to make sure it’s the right fit.

Is this an ADHD assessment?

No. I don’t diagnose or assess ADHD. The group is for people who already have a diagnosis, and it works alongside your GP and prescriber.

Can I come if I’m not taking medication?

Yes. The group works whether you take medication or not. Decisions about medication stay with you and your prescriber.

I’m pregnant or have a new baby. Is this for me?

Yes. Choose the “Pregnant or with a baby under one” door on the form. Babies are welcome in the room, and feeding, settling and walking around are all fine.

Can I join online?

The first groups are in person in Nedlands. If you’d need online, say so on the form. It helps me see whether an online group is needed too.

Safety note

This is not a crisis service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people can contact 13YARN on 13 92 76. If you’re pregnant or in the first year with a baby, PANDA is on 1300 726 306.
