How your details are handled

Body Belonging Clinic collects your name, contact details and the preferences above so I can plan in person times and contact you when they open. Apart from how you hope to pay, please don’t include health information here. If you’d like to start sooner, or something feels urgent, send an enquiry or email the clinic and I’ll be in touch.

Your details are only used for this list and aren’t shared with anyone. The form is processed by our website host, whose servers may be outside Australia. I’ll keep your details until in person times are offered, and remove them if you let me know you no longer need a place, or if we haven’t heard back eight weeks after times open. Our Privacy policy explains how to access or correct your information, or make a complaint.

Safety note

This is not a crisis service.

Body Belonging Clinic is not an emergency or crisis service, and this list is not monitored at all times. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support in Australia, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people can contact 13YARN on 13 92 76. Children and young people can contact Kids Helpline on 1800 55 1800. For eating disorder support, contact the Butterfly Foundation National Helpline on 1800 33 4673.

Body Belonging Clinic acknowledges Country.

Based in Boorloo on Whadjuk Noongar Country, with telehealth support across Australia. We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters we work on, and pay respect to Elders past and present. We recognise that body, eating, health, identity, culture and belonging are connected, and that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples hold continuing relationships to Country, community, kin and culture.

Sovereignty was never ceded. Always was, always will be Aboriginal land.
