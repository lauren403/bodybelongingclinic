One story, not two

Made for the way your brain actually works.

If you’re neurodivergent, eating and the brain are often the same story: interoception that runs quiet, sensory needs and safe foods, an appetite that vanishes on stimulant meds and arrives all at once after dark. We work right where ADHD, autism and AuDHD meet disordered eating, ARFID and body image: the place other services tend to split apart.

Fees & Medicare

Clear fees, room to make it work.

First session, 60 minutes, $200. Ongoing sessions, 55 minutes, $200. With a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan, Medicare pays $89.50 back on each session, so you pay $110.50, for up to 10 sessions a year. Some people are eligible for an Eating Disorder Plan, with up to 40 rebated sessions in 12 months.

  • No GP plan? Book privately. No referral needed.
  • Have a GP plan, or want one? Book the same way, and we’ll help you sort the right plan so the rebate applies.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need a referral to start?

No. You can book privately without one. A GP Mental Health Treatment Plan or Eating Disorder Plan is only needed to access Medicare rebates.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Telehealth across Australia, with in person times in Perth being planned. In-person times are arranged with the clinic rather than booked online, so just ask.

How much is a session?

The standard 55-minute session is $200, with Medicare rebates for eligible clients; out-of-pocket costs apply. See full fees & how to begin →

Who do you work with?

Children, adolescents and adults, with family-aware care where it helps, wherever ADHD, autism, eating and body image meet.
