Fees & Medicare

Fees, rebates and access.

The standard 55-minute session is $200. A limited number of reduced-fee concession places are available for clients experiencing financial hardship — just ask.

Eligible clients may be able to access Medicare rebates with a valid GP Mental Health Treatment Plan, psychiatrist or paediatrician referral, or an eligible Medicare Eating Disorder Treatment and Management Plan. Out-of-pocket costs may apply.

Quick Chair — our 25-minute telehealth session — is bulk-billed for eligible clients with a Mental Health Treatment Plan and a current referral, so there’s nothing to pay. Because it depends on your Medicare plan, we arrange it with you rather than have you book it online. Email the clinic, or start with a free 15-minute intro call — that needs no plan and no referral, and we can help you sort one from there.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much is a session?

The standard 55-minute session is $200, with Medicare rebates for eligible clients. Concession places and bulk-billing for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander clients are covered in Fees & Medicare above.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book privately without one. A referral or plan is only needed to access Medicare rebates (see Fees & Medicare above).

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes — telehealth across Australia, with in-person sessions in Nedlands from November 2026.

What happens in a first session?

A gentle conversation about what’s happening now, what you’re hoping for, and what might help, at your pace. There’s no pressure to have it all worked out before you arrive.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Your information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles and Accredited Mental Health Social Worker (AMHSW) practice responsibilities, and we only communicate with your GP or care team with your consent. See our Privacy policy.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. We’re currently welcoming new clients. Book online, or email the clinic if you’d like to ask about fit first.

Safety note

This is not a crisis service.

Body Belonging Clinic is not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support in Australia, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people can contact 13YARN on 13 92 76. Children and young people can contact Kids Helpline on 1800 55 1800. For eating disorder information and support, contact the Butterfly Foundation on 1800 33 4673.
